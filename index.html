03 — ENGINEERING CASE STUDY

From breadboard to working prototype.

The final system uses temperature and motion sensing — no IR obstacle sensor. It measures temperature periodically and activates the LED and active buzzer when the PIR output signals motion.

Current stageFunctional subsystems · integration testing
CONTROLLERELEGOO Uno R3
INPUTSDHT11 + PIR
OUTPUTSLED + buzzer
DISPLAY20×2 parallel LCD
01

Design objective

Create a low-cost monitoring prototype that reports temperature and motion status and produces a clear local alert when movement is detected.

02

Implementation

Integrated a DHT11 and PIR with an Uno R3. The Arduino reads the inputs, drives an LED and a two-pin active buzzer, and updates an HD44780-style LCD in 4-bit mode.

03

Troubleshooting

Corrected a sensor wiring issue that prevented temperature readings; isolated LED and buzzer circuits to validate outputs; investigated LCD contrast and PIR response timing.

04

Next iteration

Test detection range and response times, document reliability, and design a SolidWorks enclosure with accessible mounting and cable routing.
